Clean and repair Arabic

Arabic text cleaner

Paste messy Arabic and get consistent spacing, line breaks, and punctuation without editing every line by hand.

Rules run only when you press Clean text.
Cleanup rules

The comma rule leaves decimal numbers alone: 3,14 and 1,250.00 are not treated as sentence commas.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Arabic text rarely arrives clean. It is copied from a PDF, pasted from a chat, exported from a spreadsheet, or dictated into a phone. The words are correct, but the layout is not: double spaces, stray line breaks, Latin commas inside Arabic sentences, and punctuation glued to the next word. Reading is possible; publishing is not.

This cleaner applies four targeted fixes. Each one can be switched off, so you can remove only the problem you actually have. The text never leaves your browser.

What the cleaner fixes

  • Repeated spaces and tabs become a single space. This is the most common issue after a PDF copy.
  • Runs of blank lines are reduced to one empty line, keeping paragraph breaks while removing page-break noise.
  • Latin punctuation is converted to Arabic forms: comma to ،, question mark to ؟, and semicolon to ؛.
  • Punctuation spacing is normalized: no space before a mark, one space after it.

The order matters. Spaces are collapsed first, punctuation is converted second, and spacing is fixed last. That sequence avoids creating new double spaces after a comma has been replaced.

Commas and decimals

A blind comma replacement would destroy numbers. In English and French, a comma can be a decimal separator or a thousands separator. The cleaner uses a lookaround rule: a comma is converted only when it is not surrounded by digits. That means 3,14 and 1,250.00 stay untouched, while the comma in مرحبا، كيف حالك becomes Arabic.

If your document mixes French decimal commas and Arabic sentence commas, check a sample after cleaning. When in doubt, turn off the punctuation rule and convert sentence commas with the punctuation converter, where you can see the result live.

Line breaks from PDFs and emails

PDF extraction often inserts a line break at the end of every visual line. The cleaner reduces three or more newlines to two, but it does not join wrapped lines automatically. Joining is risky because Arabic paragraphs legitimately start on a new line. If you need to rebuild paragraphs, use the find and replace tool to remove single newlines, then restore paragraph breaks manually.

Why spacing around punctuation matters

Arabic typesetting follows the same basic rule as Latin: punctuation attaches to the preceding word, with a space after it. A comma floating between two words looks like a separate token and can confuse search, screen readers, and word counters. The cleaner removes the space before ، ؛ ؟ and adds a space after, but it avoids adding a space before a closing bracket or quote.

A short before-and-after

Before

كان الجو جميلا , فقررنا الخروج ; لكننا عدنا سريعا ؟

After

كان الجو جميلا، فقررنا الخروج؛ لكننا عدنا سريعا؟

The words are identical. Only spaces and punctuation changed, which is exactly the point: the cleaner should not rewrite your Arabic, only its presentation.

When not to clean

Do not run the punctuation rule on source code, URLs, or bilingual tables where the Latin comma is part of the data. For those, disable punctuation conversion and use only the space and line-break rules. The checkboxes exist so the tool can be as narrow as the job requires.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Does the cleaner change the meaning of my text?

No. It only changes whitespace and punctuation. Words and letters are untouched, and every rule can be disabled before you run it.

Will it break decimal numbers that use a comma?

No. A comma between two digits is left alone, so 3,14 and 1,250.00 survive. Only commas used as sentence punctuation are converted to the Arabic comma.

Can it join lines that were split by a PDF?

It reduces long runs of blank lines but does not join wrapped lines automatically. Use find and replace to remove single newlines, then add paragraph breaks where needed.

Is the cleaning reversible?

Not automatically. Keep a copy of the original if you may need it. The editor itself does not keep a history, and reloading the page clears it.

Does it work with mixed Arabic and English?

Yes, but decide about punctuation first. Disable Latin-to-Arabic punctuation conversion if the English sentences need to keep Latin commas and question marks.
