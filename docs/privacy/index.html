Privacy

Privacy Policy

Arabic Keyboard Online does not collect, transmit, or store the text you enter. Every tool on this site runs as JavaScript inside your browser, so your words never reach a server.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for an account, an email address, or a payment method. We do not upload your text, files, or images to any database. We do not sell data.

External services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests send standard technical information such as your IP address to Google, as any website request does. When you open a link to Google Search, YouTube, or another site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Local browser storage

The tools do not save your text in cookies or local storage. Closing or reloading the page clears the editor. Clipboard access happens only when you press a copy button.

Contact

This is a free static site published with GitHub Pages. For privacy questions about GitHub's own logging, see GitHub's privacy statement.
